## 2. Full Description (Google Play)

**CIRK** (pronounced “sirk”)  
**Your people. Your network. Anywhere.**

Connect with your friends, campus group, festival crew, or neighborhood when cell towers are congested, mobile data is down, or internet access is completely unavailable.

CIRK is an offline-first, decentralized peer-to-peer mesh networking app that turns smartphones into a resilient local network. Powered by Android Nearby Connections, CIRK automatically relays public announcements and updates from phone to phone across the crowd.

### WHY CIRK?

• REAL MULTI-HOP RELAYING: Messages travel through chains of intermediate devices (A → B → C → D). Receive community updates even when you are far beyond the original sender's direct Bluetooth or Wi-Fi range.

• BUILT FOR EVENTS & COMMUNITIES: Perfect for crowded music festivals, sporting events, outdoor campus groups, rallies, camping trips, and neighborhood resilience during power outages.

• 100% OFFLINE & SERVERLESS: No central servers, cloud databases, SIM cards, cellular data, or internet connectivity needed.

• NO ACCOUNTS OR PASSWORDS: Pick an alias and jump in. No phone numbers, email addresses, or social media logins required.

• HOST DISCONNECT TOLERANCE: If the original network creator leaves or loses power, authorized relay nodes continue forwarding bulletins and keeping the CIRK alive.

• SECURE OFFLINE QR INVITATIONS: Invite nearby people instantly using offline QR codes with scoped member or relay delegation roles.

• STORE & FORWARD: Reconnected members automatically catch up on recent community announcements.

• BATTERY-CONSCIOUS: Low-power background discovery preserves battery life for all-day events.
